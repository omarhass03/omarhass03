# Omar Hassan

Behavioural and health economist. M.Sc. in Economics and Finance, Barcelona School of Economics (2026; thesis 8.7/10).

I study what people know about how their own preferences are formed, and how the reasons they give for their choices change what they believe and do next. Health care is where I apply it: whether patients who are priced out of a treatment come to believe they need it less.

## Work

- [**The Stranger Within**](https://github.com/omarhass03/stranger-within): can people tell which of their own choices are about to reverse? An online experiment (N = 76) in which 53% of choice-price pairs reversed, 94% in the predicted direction, while participants showed little ability to foresee their own reversals. Paper, de-identified data and the full R analysis.
- [**The Deductible as a Tax on the Sick**](https://github.com/omarhass03/deductible-tax-on-the-sick): 2018 Medical Expenditure Panel Survey data show diabetics in high-deductible health plans paying about 57% more out of pocket than diabetics in other private plans, with similar total spending. A sufficient-statistic case for covering high-value chronic care before the deductible.
